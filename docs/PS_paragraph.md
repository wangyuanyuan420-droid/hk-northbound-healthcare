# Personal Statement — Project Paragraph

### English version (~185 words)

For my undergraduate thesis I studied a live social-policy question — Hong
Kong residents travelling north to mainland China for medical care (港人北上
就醫) — using computational methods rather than a small survey alone. I built
Python scrapers (Selenium and the YouTube Data API) to assemble an original
corpus of roughly 1,500 news videos from four Hong Kong outlets, over 3,200
public comments, and 95 full news articles spanning 2018–2024. I then cleaned
and tokenised Traditional-Chinese and Cantonese text with jieba and
PyCantonese, ran SnowNLP sentiment analysis, LDA topic modelling, and
time-trend analysis. The data revealed how coverage collapsed during the
pandemic border closure and surged when the boundary reopened in 2023, and
that public sentiment was markedly more critical than the news framing,
centred on cost of living and cross-boundary consumption. Rebuilding the
project as a reproducible, anonymised GitHub repository taught me as much
about research ethics and engineering rigour as about the topic itself. This
combination — a real social-science question, large-scale naturally occurring
data, and computational analysis — is exactly what I hope to deepen in the
MSc in Social Data Analytics.

### 中文参考版（约 190 字）

本科毕业论文中，我以"港人北上就醫"这一现实社会政策议题为对象，尝试用计算方法
而非单一问卷来研究它。我用 Python（Selenium 与 YouTube Data API）编写爬虫，
自建了一个原始语料库：约 1,500 条来自四家香港媒体的新闻视频、逾 3,200 条公开
评论，以及 95 篇完整新闻，时间跨度 2018–2024 年。随后我用 jieba 与
PyCantonese 处理繁体中文及粤语文本，进行 SnowNLP 情感分析、LDA 主题建模与时间
趋势分析。数据显示：疫情封关期间报道几近消失，2023 年通关后骤然激增；公众情绪
明显比新闻框架更为负面，且高度聚焦于生活成本与跨境消费。将项目重构为可复现、
已匿名化的 GitHub 仓库，让我在研究伦理与工程规范上收获与议题本身同样多。社会科学
议题、大规模自然数据与计算方法的结合，正是我希望在社会数据分析硕士阶段进一步深化的。
